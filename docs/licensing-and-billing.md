## When a subscription lapses

This is the part worth reading carefully, because it is where most software is unpleasant and this
one tries not to be.

**What you keep, permanently:**

- Every application Siklab has built for you — source, assets, documentation
- The right to use, modify, host, distribute and **sell** all of it
- Access to open, run and export everything already on your machine

**What stops:**

- **Starting** new builds
- **Installing updates** — the app keeps running at the version you have

Nothing is deleted, nothing is locked, nothing phones home to take anything away. A lapsed licence
means the factory stops taking new orders; it does not repossess what it already made.

Renewing restores both immediately.

## How the licence is checked

Honest mechanics, because guessing at this is worse than knowing.

Your install holds a signed licence with a **short lease** that renews automatically in the
background while your subscription is active. The renewal is quiet and needs nothing from you.

If the machine cannot reach the licence service, the lease keeps working until it expires — a flight,
a bad hotel network or a day offline will not lock you out. What it will not do is grant an
indefinite licence to an install that never checks in.

## What you own, precisely

**Yours** — applications, source code, assets and documentation that Siklab produces for you,
**including anything built during the trial**. Use, modify, host, distribute and sell freely. No
royalty, no attribution, no Siklab component required to run them.

**Not yours** — Siklab Core itself: the application, its agents, prompts, orchestration, governance
logic, templates and activation code. You may run it under licence. You may not redistribute it,
resell it, host it as a service for other people, or take it apart to extract its internals.

**One honest caveat** — generated projects commonly pull in third-party libraries under their own
licences. Siklab cannot grant rights it does not have. Check those before shipping commercially; the
full third-party notice ships inside the app.
